Update on ICF Coach Draft Pad Dimensions Standardized to 32.0 mm

May 22, 2026 By Union Tech Wing 1,240 Views

The Railway Board has issued a technical clarification regarding the maintenance standards for Integral Coach Factory (ICF) design coaches. Following representations and technical reviews by the engineering departments, the standard measurements for critical suspension components have been officially updated in the manual.

It has been noted that there was prior confusion regarding the draft pad dimensions in the bogie assembly. To ensure uniformity and optimal safety performance across all divisions, the measurements have been standardized.

"Effective immediately, the new thickness standard for draft pads in ICF coaches is officially verified and set at 32.0 mm. All maintenance depots must update their technical sheets accordingly."

Preparation for Upcoming Examinations

For staff currently preparing for the departmental JE Mechanical examinations, this updated metric is highly relevant. Educational materials, MCQs, and technical data sheets distributed by the union's education wing have been amended to reflect this 32.0 mm standard. Candidates are advised to note this correction for objective-type questions regarding rolling stock dimensions.

A complete tab-separated PDF containing updated MCQs for rolling stock maintenance will be uploaded to the portal's document section shortly. We request all branch secretaries to ensure this bulletin reaches the workshop floor staff immediately.
